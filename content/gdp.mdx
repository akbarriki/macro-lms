## What is GDP?

**Gross domestic product (GDP)** is the market value of all final goods and
services produced within a country in a given period of time.

## Four types of spending

Economists often add up GDP by looking at who is spending:

- **Consumption (C):** spending by households
- **Investment (I):** spending by firms on things like equipment and buildings, changes in inventories, and spending on new homes
- **Government purchases (G):** government spending on goods and services
- **Net exports (NX):** exports minus imports

Together: **GDP = C + I + G + NX**

## What GDP leaves out

GDP does not count unpaid work at home, and it does not show how income is
shared. A higher GDP does not always mean that everyone is better off.
